Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

1. Information we collect

When you sign up we store your name and email. Game progress, purchases and sign-in status are stored in your own browser (localStorage) on this demo site.

2. How we use it

To run your account, unlock premium games, show your best scores and improve GameVerse. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Cookies and local storage

We use browser storage to keep you signed in and remember what you own. You can clear it anytime from your browser settings.

4. Payments

Payments are processed by a payment provider. GameVerse does not store full card details. The checkout on this version is a demo.

5. Children

Kids games are family friendly. Children under 13 should use GameVerse with a parent or guardian.

6. Your rights

You can ask to view, correct or delete your data through Contact Us.

7. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.

This is a sample policy. Have it reviewed by a legal professional before launching.
